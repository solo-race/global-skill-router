# Exploratory Work

Start with a bounded question and the evidence needed; read-only inspection is the
default. Use scout or a specialist only when useful. Report observations, hypotheses,
unknowns and the smallest next decision separately.

Within existing task authority, allow up to two small local reproductions or diagnostic
experiments in an isolated disposable workspace. Bound the question, commands and
resources to that task; stop when answered or the experiment budget is exhausted.
No separate experiment approval form is needed for these already-authorized local
checks. This allowance neither grants mutation rights to read-only roles nor overrides
user/host time, cost, tool, quota or permission limits. A temporary directory is not
permission to touch real services.

External transmission, production effects, installation, indexing, permission changes,
real-data mutation or work outside existing authority is excluded from this allowance.
Obtain the applicable separate bounded authorization before those actions, including
scope, resource limits, validation, safe cleanup/rollback and stop conditions. Do not
silently broaden a local reproduction into a live-system test.

Count exploratory passes and experiments across handoffs and mode changes. Failed
experiments return evidence and use only safe authorized cleanup; stop and report
unsafe cleanup. Negative/partial graph results are not absence. Findings may propose
Direct or Planned only when target scope, authority and review requirements pass.
Unresolved questions at budget exhaustion -> BLOCKED, not endless investigation.
